Game Mastering: What Is a Challenge?

KidzMash: Roll your own tabletop role playing game!

What is a challenge?

A challenge is any point during the game when you want to see if a character is able to complete the action they're trying to do, and there is a chance of success or failure.

Based on how the character wants to approach the challenge determines how the challenge pool is made.

The dice help determine what happens. The Game Master decides what that result means in the story.

One Challenge, Different Approaches

Imagine this: Your instructor is sitting in a chair. Could one kid lift the instructor and the chair? Maybe—but the odds aren't very good!

Approach 1: Strength

One kid tries to lift the chair alone. That sounds like a difficult Strength challenge.

Approach 2: Smarts

Another kid says, “Hey, let's get four people together to lift!” That's a clever plan—Smarts, not just Strength.

Does the group still need to roll? Maybe not! A good plan can make a challenge easier—or remove the need for a roll altogether.

This is a thought experiment. No actual chair lifting required!

What Does Failure Look Like?

Even an adult who could lift the chair might pull a muscle while trying. A failed roll doesn't always have to mean “nothing happens.” It might mean the character struggles, gets hurt, or succeeds with a complication.

Try It in Your Game

Think of one challenge a character might face. How could they solve it in two different ways?

Game Master tip: Ask players “What do you try?” before deciding whether a roll is needed.
