Roll your own tabletop role playing game!

KidzMash · Design Your Game's Abilities

Build your game's ability system! Start with a general pool. You can stop there and have a simple game, or add attributes, skills, and traits to give characters special strengths. Circle names you like, write your own, or cross out any group you don't want.

1 · Start with a General Pool

Every character starts with a General Dice Pool or General Ability Cube Pool to try challenges. Circle one:

How to build a pool for a challenge: General Pool + relevant Attribute + relevant Skill or Trait = Total dice or ability cubes.
Example: General (3) + Strength (2) + Climbing (1) = 6 dice or ability cubes. Add bonuses only when they make sense for the challenge.

2 · Optional Add-on: Attributes

Attributes describe broad abilities. If you use them, their values add to the General Pool when relevant. Choose a name for each group you want, or write your own. Don't want a group? Cross it out! You can use one, a few, all six, or none.

3 · Optional Add-on: Skills & Traits

Skills and traits are specific things a character is good at. Like attributes, they add dice or ability cubes to the General Pool when they help with a challenge. Think of ideas for your game:

Example: Climbing, Tracking, Sneaking, Cooking, Animal Friend
