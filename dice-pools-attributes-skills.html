Design Your Challenge & Conflict Resolution System

KidzMash: Roll your own tabletop role playing game!

Decide how your game handles challenges! When your game requires a challenge or conflict resolution, characters can use a General Pool to determine the outcome. You can stop there, or let attributes, skills, and traits add dice or ability cubes when relevant. This page sets the rules; players assign their character points during character creation.

How to Build a Pool for a Challenge
General Pool + relevant Attribute points (optional) + relevant Skill or Trait points (optional) = Total dice or ability cubes.
General Pool only: General (3) = 3 dice or ability cubes.
General + Attribute: General (3) + Strength (2) = 5 dice or ability cubes.
General + Attribute + Skill: General (3) + Strength (2) + Climbing (1) = 6 dice or ability cubes.
These numbers are examples, not fixed bonuses. Players assign attribute and skill points during character creation. Add only bonuses relevant to the challenge.

2 · Optional Add-on: Attributes

Attributes describe broad abilities. If you include them, points assigned to relevant attributes add to the General Pool. Players assign those points during character creation. Choose a name for each group you want, or write your own. Don't want a group? Cross it out! You can use one, a few, all six, or none.

3 · Optional Add-on: Skills & Traits

Skills and traits are specific talents or characteristics. If included, points assigned to relevant skills or traits add dice or ability cubes when resolving a challenge. Players assign those points during character creation. Think of ideas for your game.

Examples: Climbing, Tracking, Sneaking, Cooking, Animal Friend
