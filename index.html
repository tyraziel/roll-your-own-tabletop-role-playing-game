Roll Your Own Tabletop Role Playing Game!

KidzMash · Workshop resources

Design your own tabletop role playing game! Choose how your game handles challenges, build characters and creatures, and learn how to guide the story as a Game Master.

Workshop Modules

Work through these activities as you create your game.

Design Your Challenge & Conflict Resolution System Choose a general pool and optionally add attributes, skills, and traits. Game Mastering: What Is a Challenge? Explore different approaches to challenges and what the results mean in the story.
Character Creation Build a character and assign attributes, skills, and traits.
Creature & Monster Creation Invent creatures and monsters for your game.
World & Setting Creation Imagine where your adventures take place.
Scenario Building Create an adventure with obstacles, choices, and goals.
Playtesting & Refinement Try your game, learn what works, and make adjustments.
