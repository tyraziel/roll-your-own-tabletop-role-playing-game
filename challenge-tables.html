Roll your own tabletop role playing game!

A KidzMash, hands-on creative workshop

Choose a way to resolve challenges: dice pools or a colored-cube draw. For dice, roll 1–15 D6 or D10 and use difficulty 1–10 as the number of successes required; each percentage shows the chance of rolling at least that many successes. For cubes, put 1–15 ability (success) cubes and 1–10 difficulty cubes in a bag, mix, and draw one at random. Drawing an ability-colored cube means success. The cube table shows the chance of drawing that color.

Suggested Difficulty Scale

Use these descriptions to help decide how challenging an action should be.

Probability color bands

Very likely ≥85%Likely 65–<85%Moderate 40–<65%Unlikely 20–<40%Very unlikely <20%

These colors describe the chance of success for a particular dice pool or cube combination—not a fixed difficulty level. In the dice tables, a dash (—) means the required successes exceed the dice available, so success is impossible. The cube table always has a numeric probability because both colors are present. Values below 0.01% are shown as <0.01%, not zero.

Table E — Colored Cube Draw

Rows = success cubes (ability, 1–15). Columns = difficulty cubes (1–10). Mix both colors in a bag and draw one cube. A success-colored cube means success.

One cube is drawn at random; all cubes have equal odds of being drawn. Use the suggested difficulty scale above as a reference.

Chance of success = success cubes ÷ (success cubes + difficulty cubes) × 100%. Example: 5 success cubes and 5 difficulty cubes = 50%.
